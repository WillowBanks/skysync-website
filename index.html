How it works

Spatial awareness in seconds

No geospatial degree required. No IT setup. Three steps from raw footage to a full picture of your site.

01
📂
Drop in your files
Drag your DJI footage folder straight into SkySync. It reads the embedded GPS, timestamps, and telemetry directly from the files — no conversion, no pre-processing. MP4, MOV, LAS/LAZ from any drone that logs GPS.
02
🔌
Point it at your data
Paste in a REST endpoint, a CSV with coordinates, a GeoJSON, or an ArcGIS Server URL. If your organisation has an asset register, inspection database, or monitoring feed — SkySync reads it. Bring your own data source. No reformatting.
03
🎯
Click the map, be there
Click any point on the map — SkySync jumps the video to the closest matching frame. Scrub the video — the map cursor follows. Every flag and note is pinned to a real-world coordinate. You never guess what you're looking at again.
